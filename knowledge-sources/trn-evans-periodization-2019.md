## Should you periodize your training? Periodized always beats random/non-progressive
tags: periodization, program, programming, mesocycle, deload, strength, hypertrophy, muscle, progression

Structured periodization consistently outperforms non-periodized (random or constant-load) training for maximal strength — multiple meta-analyses reviewed here all agree on this. For hypertrophy the evidence is less clear-cut, but having any structured, progressive plan still outperforms training without one. Periodization works by systematically varying training stress (load, volume, intensity, or rep range) to drive continued adaptation, prevent plateaus, and manage fatigue. Even a simple scheme — 4–6 weeks of moderate reps (8–12), then 4–6 weeks of lower reps (4–6), then a deload week — qualifies as linear periodization and yields better long-term strength outcomes than doing the same workout indefinitely.
