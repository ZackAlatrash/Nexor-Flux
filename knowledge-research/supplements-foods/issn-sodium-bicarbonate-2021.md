# ISSN Position Stand: Sodium Bicarbonate and Exercise Performance (2021)

**Full citation:** Grgic J, Pedisic Z, Saunders B, Artioli GG, Schoenfeld BJ, McKenna MJ, Bishop DJ, Kreider RB, Stout JR, Kalman DS, Arent SM, VanDusseldorp TA, Lopez HL, Ziegenfuss TN, Burke LM, Antonio J, Campbell BI. **Source URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8427947/
**Access:** open access (CC BY 4.0)

## What this source covers
ISSN position stand on sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO3) as an ergogenic aid, covering dosing protocols, timing strategies, exercise-type specificity, side effects, and mitigation strategies. Based on analysis of 125+ studies.

## Key findings & numbers
- Single-dose protocol: 0.3 g/kg body weight (optimal; 0.2 g/kg minimum to detect ergogenic effects)
- Multi-day loading protocol: 0.4–0.5 g/kg/day for 3–7 days (achieves similar blood buffering with reduced GI side effects)
- Timing: 60–180 minutes before exercise for single-dose; peak blood buffering occurs ~60–90 min post-ingestion
- Exercise types and durations that benefit: high-intensity efforts lasting 30 seconds to 12 minutes
- Specific sports with documented benefits: boxing, judo, karate, taekwondo, wrestling; high-intensity cycling, running, swimming (200–400 m events), rowing (2000 m)
- Mechanism: attenuates accumulation of lactate and H+ ions via extracellular buffering; most effective for glycolytic metabolic demands
- Side effects: bloating, nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain; generally low incidence but variable between individuals
- Side effect mitigation strategies:
  1. Reduce dose to 0.2–0.3 g/kg
  2. Time ingestion ~180 minutes before exercise
  3. Consume with high-carbohydrate meal
  4. Use enteric-coated capsules
- Blood lactate: elevated during and after high-intensity exercise with sodium bicarbonate supplementation (indicating greater glycolytic flux)
- Effect on endurance events >8 minutes: limited evidence

## Direct specifics / quotes
> "For single-dose supplementation protocols, the recommended timing of sodium bicarbonate ingestion is between 60 and 180 min before exercise or competition."

## Relevance to a body-recomposition coaching app
- Evidence strength: STRONG for 30-second to 12-minute high-intensity efforts; limited for longer endurance or pure strength work
- Not typically relevant for recreational gym-goers; most relevant for competitive athletes in combat sports, rowing, track
- Practical issue: GI side effects make it impractical for many users without experimentation
- If recommending: start at 0.2 g/kg with a carbohydrate meal ~60–90 min before high-intensity efforts; use multi-day protocols to reduce GI distress
- Better tolerance strategies: enteric-coated capsules or multi-day dosing
