# Schoenfeld & Aragon 2018: How Much Protein Per Meal for Muscle Building?

**Full citation:** Schoenfeld BJ, Aragon AA. How much protein can the body use in a single meal for muscle-building? Implications for daily protein distribution. J Int Soc Sports Nutr. 2018;15:10. doi:10.1186/s12970-018-0215-1. PMID: 29497353. PMC5828430.
**Source URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5828430/
**Access:** Open access (CC BY 4.0)

## What this source covers
A review challenging the conventional "20–25 g protein per meal" ceiling, examining the true upper anabolic threshold for per-meal protein intake and deriving practical daily protein distribution guidelines.

## Key findings & numbers
- Early studies proposed MPS is maximized at **20–25 g of fast-digesting protein** per meal in young adults.
- Newer evidence: **40 g whey** produced ~**20% greater myofibrillar MPS** than 20 g over the 12 hours post-exercise.
- Mixed meals containing **40–70 g of beef protein** showed dose-dependent anabolic responses (slower digestion rate extends amino acid availability window).
- Practical recommendation: **0.4 g/kg per meal** across a minimum of **4 meals** daily = 1.6 g/kg/day minimum.
- Upper range: **0.55 g/kg per meal** = 2.2 g/kg/day across 4 meals.
- Older adults may require up to **0.60 g/kg per meal** (higher leucine threshold with age).
- Some ingested AAs above the 20–25 g threshold ARE utilized for tissue-building, not just oxidized.
- Chronic longitudinal studies have not consistently corroborated acute MPS study results — distribution may matter less at the level of weeks/months than daily totals.

## Direct specifics / quotes
> "While research shows that consumption of higher protein doses (> 20 g) results in greater AA oxidation, evidence indicates that this is not the fate for all the additional ingested AAs as some are utilized for tissue-building purposes."

> "Consumption of slower-acting protein sources, particularly when consumed with other macronutrients, would delay absorption and thus conceivably enhance utilization of amino acids."

## Relevance to a body-recomposition coaching app
- Revise the old "20–25 g hard ceiling per meal" rule: larger meals (40+ g protein) with slower-digesting proteins or in mixed-food context DO provide additional anabolic stimulus.
- The 0.4 g/kg per meal × 4 meals = 1.6 g/kg/day framework is practical and conservative.
- For users eating 3 meals/day, each meal can profitably contain 40–50 g protein.
- Distribute protein across at least 4 daily eating occasions to ensure leucine threshold is hit repeatedly throughout the day.
- Daily protein total remains the primary lever; meal distribution is secondary but worth optimizing when users are already hitting their daily target.
