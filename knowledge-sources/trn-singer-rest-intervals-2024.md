## How long to rest between sets for muscle growth
tags: rest, "rest-interval", "rest-time", hypertrophy, muscle, sets, gains, "how-long"

Rest 90–120 seconds between sets for hypertrophy — this is the practical sweet spot supported by a Bayesian meta-analysis of 9 RCTs (19 measurements). The analysis found that intermediate rest (61–119 s) produced the peak hypertrophic response (SMD = 0.65), outperforming both short rest (≤60 s, SMD = 0.47) and very long rest (≥180 s, SMD = 0.50). Resting longer than 90 seconds beyond that intermediate zone offers no additional hypertrophic benefit. The old guideline of 30–90 seconds for "hypertrophy-specific" training (previously promoted by NSCA) appears suboptimal — short rest compromises volume load and therefore the hypertrophic stimulus. For most working sets, a 90–120 second timer is practical and evidence-based.
