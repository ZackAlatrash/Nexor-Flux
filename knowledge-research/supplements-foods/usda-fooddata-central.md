# USDA FoodData Central — Nutrient Database

**Full citation:** USDA Agricultural Research Service. FoodData Central. United States Department of Agriculture. Available at: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/. Data current through April 2026.
**Source URL:** https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/
**Access:** public domain (CC0 1.0 Universal — no copyright restrictions)

## What this source covers
USDA FoodData Central is the official U.S. government nutrient composition database providing macronutrient and micronutrient data for generic and branded foods. It is public domain (CC0) and available via web search, API, and bulk download. It is the gold-standard food composition database for English-language nutritional applications.

## Key findings & numbers

### Database Coverage (5 data types)
1. **Foundation Foods** — Analytical data on commodity and minimally processed food samples; updated semi-annually (April and October); most rigorous methodology
2. **SR Legacy (Standard Reference)** — Historical nutrient data from analyses, calculations, and published literature; final update April 2018; no longer updated but still widely used reference
3. **Survey Foods (FNDDS)** — Food and Nutrient Database for Dietary Studies; used for NHANES national surveys; updated every 2 years; latest: October 2024
4. **Experimental Foods** — Peer-reviewed research data from academic collaborations; updated twice annually when data available
5. **Branded Foods** — Commercial product nutrition label data from manufacturers; updated monthly; largest by count; latest: April 2026

### Access Methods
- **Web search**: fdc.nal.usda.gov — free, no login required
- **API**: RESTful JSON API; free; requires data.gov API key; can retrieve single food records or search results programmatically
- **Bulk download**: CSV and JSON formats; full dataset or by data type; no login required
- **SR Legacy** also available in older MS Access format (discontinued October 2021)

### Nutrient Coverage
Each food record tracks: calories (kcal), total protein (g), total fat (g), total carbohydrate (g), fiber (g), sugars (g), saturated/mono/polyunsaturated fats, cholesterol, sodium, potassium, calcium, iron, magnesium, phosphorus, zinc, vitamins A/C/D/E/K, B vitamins, amino acid profiles (Foundation Foods), fatty acid profiles

### Update Schedule
- Foundation Foods & Branded: semi-annual (April and December)
- FNDDS: biennial (every 2 years with NHANES cycles)
- SR Legacy: no further updates planned

## Relevance to a body-recomposition coaching app
- This is the primary data source for any food logging feature requiring nutrient data on generic whole foods
- Public domain (CC0) means it can be embedded in an app database without licensing concerns
- Foundation Foods provides the highest-quality analytical data for common whole foods (chicken breast, eggs, oats, etc.)
- Branded Foods enables barcode-scanning and branded product lookup
- The API enables real-time food lookups; the bulk download enables offline food library
- SR Legacy FNDDS ID numbers are widely used in third-party databases and remain the de facto standard for food ID cross-referencing
