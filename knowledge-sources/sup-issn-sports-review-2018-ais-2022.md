## Which supplements are actually proven to work — evidence tiers
tags: supplement, supplements, evidence, effective, "worth-it", safe, creatine, caffeine, "beta-alanine"
Two independent expert bodies — the International Society of Sports Nutrition (ISSN 2018) and the Australian Institute of Sport (AIS 2022) — classify supplements into evidence tiers. Their top-tier lists are nearly identical.

**ISSN Category A / AIS Group A — STRONG evidence; safe and effective when used correctly:**
- **Creatine monohydrate:** 3–5 g/day maintenance; optional 20 g/day × 5 days loading.
- **Caffeine:** 3–6 mg/kg ~60 min pre-exercise.
- **Sodium bicarbonate:** 0.3 g/kg ~60–180 min pre-exercise; high-intensity 30 s–12 min efforts only.
- **Beta-alanine:** 4–6 g/day in divided doses ≤2 g; needs 4+ weeks; best for 1–4 min high-intensity work.
- **Protein supplements** (whey, casein, soy): when whole food cannot meet daily protein targets.
- **Dietary nitrate / beetroot juice** (AIS Group A): pre-exercise nitric oxide support; especially aerobic endurance.

These are the **only** supplements that should be proactively recommended for body recomposition or performance.

## Which supplements have limited or no evidence
tags: supplement, supplements, evidence, "worth-it", hmb, citrulline, safe
**ISSN Category B / AIS Group B — LIMITED or MIXED evidence (discuss with caveats if user asks):**
- HMB: more consistent for beginners and recovery; mixed in trained athletes.
- Citrulline / citrulline malate: may modestly help late-set muscular endurance; no aerobic benefit.
- BCAAs: insufficient evidence for hypertrophy benefit beyond adequate total protein intake.
- Beetroot juice / nitrate (ISSN B): emerging evidence; AIS upgraded to Group A.

**ISSN Category C — LITTLE TO NO evidence; NOT recommended:**
- Glutamine: no hypertrophy benefit in healthy exercising adults.
- Tribulus terrestris: no ergogenic effect documented.
- Most thermogenics beyond caffeine.
- Most proprietary "proprietary blend" products.

**AIS Group D — BANNED or HIGH contamination risk:**
- Prohormones, SARMs, ephedra, and other WADA-prohibited substances. Never recommend.
