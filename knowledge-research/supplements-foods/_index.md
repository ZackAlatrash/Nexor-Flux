## Evidence Strength Summary (as stated by sources)

| Supplement / Topic | Evidence Strength | Primary Source |
|---|---|---|
| Creatine monohydrate | **STRONG** (1,000+ studies; ISSN "most effective ergogenic supplement available") | `issn-creatine-2017.md` |
| Caffeine | **STRONG** (consistent moderate-to-large benefits; ISSN, AIS Group A) | `issn-caffeine-2021.md` |
| Beta-alanine | **STRONG** for 1–4 min high-intensity; limited for longer efforts | `issn-beta-alanine-2015.md` |
| Protein supplements | **STRONG** (when whole food intake is insufficient; all ISSN stands) | `issn-protein-exercise-2017.md` |
| Sodium bicarbonate | **STRONG** for 30 s–12 min high-intensity; limited for longer efforts | `issn-sodium-bicarbonate-2021.md` |
| Nutrient timing | **STRONG** for total daily protein; **MODERATE** for strict window timing | `issn-nutrient-timing-2017.md` |
| HMB | **MODERATE** (recovery/damage attenuation); **MIXED** in trained athletes | `issn-hmb-2024.md`, `issn-hmb-2013.md` |
| Energy drinks | **MODERATE-TO-STRONG** for caffeine content; **MIXED** for multi-ingredient | `issn-energy-drinks-2023.md` |
| Citrulline malate | **LIMITED/MIXED** (some muscular endurance benefit; mechanisms unconfirmed) | `citrulline-malate-review-2021.md` |
| L-citrulline (endurance) | **LOW** (meta-analysis: no significant effect on TTE or TTC) | `citrulline-endurance-meta-2023.md` |
| Protein quality (DIAAS) | **STRONG** methodological consensus; DIAAS is FAO-preferred since 2013 | `protein-quality-pdcaas-diaas-2022.md` |
| USDA FoodData Central | Data source (public domain; no evidence rating applicable) | `usda-fooddata-central.md` |
