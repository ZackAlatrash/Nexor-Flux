## What is HMB and how much should I take
tags: hmb, dose, "how-much", supplement, muscle, recovery, safe
Evidence strength: MODERATE-TO-STRONG for body composition and recovery in untrained/beginner populations; MIXED in trained athletes (ISSN Category B).

HMB (beta-hydroxy-beta-methylbutyrate) is a metabolite of the amino acid leucine. It activates mTORC1 (a key muscle protein synthesis pathway) independently of leucine sensing, and reduces muscle damage markers.

**Dose (both 2013 and 2024 ISSN stands agree):** 38 mg/kg body weight/day — approximately **3 g/day** for a 75–80 kg person — divided into 2–3 servings.

**Forms:**
- **CaHMB** (calcium salt, most common): take 60–120 minutes pre-exercise.
- **HMB-FA** (free acid): faster absorption; 30–60 minutes pre-exercise; ~91–97% greater plasma area-under-the-curve than CaHMB, but less commonly available and more expensive.

**Duration:** at least 3–4 weeks to see body composition changes; trained athletes may need ≥6 weeks to show consistent benefits.

**Safety:** chronic use (up to 1 year) documented as safe with no adverse effects on blood glucose, insulin sensitivity, or kidney markers.

## Who benefits most from HMB
tags: hmb, supplement, muscle, gains, recovery, sore, soreness, beginner, advanced
Evidence strength: MODERATE; most consistent for beginners, returning athletes, and high-damage training blocks.

**Best candidates for HMB:**
- Beginners or those returning after a training break — studies in untrained individuals show fat-free mass gains of +0.4 to +2.4 kg and strength improvements of 7–45% in 3–8 week protocols.
- Anyone doing intensified training blocks with high eccentric loading — HMB reduces creatine kinase (CK) by 20–60% and LDH significantly, indicating less muscle membrane damage and faster recovery.

**Trained athletes:** evidence is mixed. Most studies show modest or no hypertrophy benefit unless duration is >6 weeks. A 2013 outlier study (Wilson et al.) reported +5.3 kg LBM over 12 weeks, which is not replicated at the same magnitude in subsequent work.

**Bottom line:** HMB is most useful for beginners, older adults (55+ years), or periods of unusually high training damage (new program, deload return, injury comeback). For experienced athletes chasing marginal gains, it is a reasonable but unproven add-on.
