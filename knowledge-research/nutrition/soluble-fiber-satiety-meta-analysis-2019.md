# Soluble Fiber and Satiety: Systematic Review & Meta-Analysis (Salleh 2019)

**Full citation:** Salleh SN, Fairus AAH, Zahary MN, Raj NB, Mhd Jalil AM. Unravelling the Effects of Soluble Dietary Fibre Supplementation on Energy Intake and Perceived Satiety in Healthy Adults: Evidence from Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomised-Controlled Trials. Foods. 2019;8(1):15. doi:10.3390/foods8010015. PMC6352252.
**Source URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6352252/
**Access:** Open access (CC BY 4.0)

## What this source covers
Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs examining how soluble dietary fiber supplementation affects energy intake and perceived satiety in healthy adults, comparing fiber types and effective doses.

## Key findings & numbers

### Fiber types ranked by satiety effect size:
1. **Guar gum:** Effect size −0.90 (strongest)
2. **β-glucan (beta-glucan):** −0.44
3. **Alginate:** −0.42
4. **Polydextrose:** −0.36
5. **Pectin:** −0.26

### Specific effective doses:
- **5 g guar gum** in milk beverages: reduced energy intake with effect size −3.55 (p<0.001)
- **9 g alginate** in chocolate cookies: effect size −0.52 (p<0.001)
- **25 g polydextrose** in liquid drinks: effect size −0.81 (p=0.01)

### Food matrix matters:
- Liquid food matrices proved more effective than solid for satiety delivery.
- Soluble fibers in liquid meals absorb more water and increase stomach distension, triggering fullness signals.

### Mechanism:
- Viscous soluble dietary fibers increase digesta viscosity → delay gastric emptying → prolonged satiety signals.

### Study quality note:
- High bias risk overall; only 1 of 17 studies was double-blinded per Jadad assessment.

## Direct specifics / quotes
> "Viscous soluble dietary fibres increase digesta viscosity and consequently delay gastric emptying."

> "Soluble fibres, when prepared in liquid meal form, absorb more water and increase stomach distension."

## Relevance to a body-recomposition coaching app
- Guar gum and beta-glucan (found in oats) are the most potent soluble fibers for hunger suppression.
- Recommend oats, psyllium husk, legumes (rich in beta-glucan and guar gum) as practical high-satiety fiber sources.
- Users struggling with hunger on a calorie deficit benefit most from high-viscosity soluble fibers.
- Fiber in liquid form (smoothies with psyllium, oat-based protein shakes) may be more satiating than the same fiber in solid form.
