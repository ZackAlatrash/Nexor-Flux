# Concurrent Training Network Meta-Analysis: HIIT Running Least Detrimental to Strength

**Full citation:** Chen Y, Feng X, Huang L, Wang K, Mi J. Comparative efficacy of concurrent training types on lower limb strength and muscular hypertrophy: A systematic review and network meta-analysis. J Exerc Sci Fit. 2024;22(1):78–86. doi:10.1016/j.jesf.2023.12.005. PMC10767279.
**Source URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10767279/
**Access:** open access (CC BY)

## What this source covers
Bayesian network meta-analysis of 40 RCTs (841 participants) comparing 4 concurrent training modalities (HIIT running, HIIT cycling, moderate-intensity continuous running, moderate-intensity continuous cycling) combined with resistance training, vs resistance training alone.

## Key findings & numbers
- **All concurrent modalities underperformed resistance training alone for lower-body maximal strength** (SUCRA = 94.0% for RT alone).
- **Lower-body maximal strength — best concurrent option:**
  - HIIRT (HIIT running + RT): SMD = −0.08 (95% CI −0.25 to 0.08), SUCRA = 71.0% — least interference.
  - MICCT (moderate continuous cycling + RT): SMD = −0.38 (95% CI −0.62 to −0.14), SUCRA = 9.7% — most interference.
- **Muscle cross-sectional area (MCSA):**
  - HIIRT + RT: SMD = 0.15 (95% CI −0.46 to 0.76), SUCRA = 67.3% — slight hypertrophy edge.
  - Hypertrophy differences between modalities were small; cardio mode less critical for muscle size than for strength.
- **Explosive strength (power):**
  - HIIRT + RT: SMD = 0.06 (95% CI −0.21 to 0.33), SUCRA = 82.7% — slight advantage over RT alone.
  - MICRT + RT: SMD = −0.37 (95% CI −0.71 to −0.04) — significant disadvantage.
- **Mechanism:** HIIT causes lower glycogen depletion than moderate continuous training (shorter duration); running recruits both concentric and eccentric contractions vs cycling's concentric-only.
- 67.5% of studies used same-session concurrent training.

## Direct specifics / quotes
> "HIIRT [HIIT running + RT] is recommended as the endurance modality...to minimize the adverse effects on lower limb strength."

> For hypertrophy: "Training modality selection is less critical when muscle cross-sectional area is the primary goal."

## Relevance to a body-recomposition coaching app
- For users doing cardio alongside lifting: **HIIT running** is the least-interference option for maintaining strength.
- Moderate-intensity continuous cycling and running show the most interference with lower-body strength.
- For recomp goals (muscle + fat loss): HIIT is superior to long slow cardio for preserving muscle.
- When users ask "can I do cardio and still build muscle?": yes, if cardio is HIIT-based and total volume is managed.
- Programming tip: if possible, do resistance training FIRST in a session, then cardio; or on separate days entirely.
