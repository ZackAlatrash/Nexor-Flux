# ISSN Position Stand: HMB (2013)

**Full citation:** Wilson JM, Fitschen PJ, Campbell B, et al. International Society of Sports Nutrition Position Stand: beta-hydroxy-beta-methylbutyrate (HMB). J Int Soc Sports Nutr. 2013;10:6. doi: 10.1186/1550-2783-10-6. PMID: 23374455
**Source URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3568064/
**Access:** open access (CC BY 2.0)

## What this source covers
ISSN position stand on HMB (a leucine metabolite) covering dosing, timing, forms (CaHMB vs HMB-FA), effects on muscle damage markers, body composition, and strength. Based on 20+ controlled studies.

## Key findings & numbers
- Recommended dose: 38 mg/kg body weight/day (typically ~3 g/day for a 80 kg person), divided into 2–3 servings
- Loading period: consume for 2 weeks prior to an exercise bout for maximal effect on muscle damage attenuation
- Timing — CaHMB: 60–120 minutes pre-exercise
- Timing — HMB-FA (free acid): 30–60 minutes pre-exercise
- Muscle damage markers reduction: creatine kinase (CK) reduced 20–60%; lactate dehydrogenase (LDH) significantly decreased; 3-methylhistidine declined ~20%
- Lean mass gains (untrained, 3-week protocol): +0.6–3.0% fat-free mass
- Strength improvements: +2.3–17.4% depending on lift type across studies
- HMB-FA bioavailability: 91–97% greater area under the curve vs. CaHMB; 25% greater plasma clearance; faster peak
- Evidence most consistent for: untrained individuals and during periods of high mechanical damage or intensified training
- Evidence in trained athletes: less consistent; benefits increase with study duration >6 weeks

## Direct specifics / quotes
> "HMB can be used to enhance recovery by attenuating exercise induced skeletal muscle damage in trained and untrained populations."

> "HMB-FA may increase plasma absorption and retention of HMB to a greater extent than HMB-CA."

## Relevance to a body-recomposition coaching app
- Evidence strength: MODERATE (consistent for recovery/muscle damage; less consistent for hypertrophy in trained athletes)
- Practical coaching guidance: 3 g/day (38 mg/kg) in divided doses; most benefit during initial training phases or high-damage training blocks
- More relevant for beginners or those returning after a layoff than for experienced lifters
- HMB-FA may be preferred form if available; most products use CaHMB
