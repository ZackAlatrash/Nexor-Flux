## Notable Disagreements Between Sources

1. **Optimal weekly set range:** Baz-Valle (2022) recommends 12–20 sets/week; Bernárdez-Vázquez umbrella (2022) says "at least 10 sets"; Iversen (2021) says minimum 4 sets is effective. These are not contradictory — they represent minimum effective, threshold, and optimal ranges respectively. The consensus zone is 10–20 direct sets/week.

2. **Training to failure:** Refalo (2022) found trivial advantage (ES 0.19) for set failure over non-failure; Robinson (2024) found a meaningful dose-response (closer to failure = more hypertrophy). Both converge on the practical recommendation of 0–3 RIR — not full failure, not excessively conservative.

3. **Rest intervals:** Singer (2024) found intermediate rest (61–119s) showed peak hypertrophic response, but the traditional NSCA guideline of 30–90s for hypertrophy appears suboptimal. Practical recommendation: 90–120s.

4. **Concurrent training interference:** Huiberts (2023) found significant lower-body strength interference in males (ES −0.43) but not females; Chen (2024) found HIIT running minimizes this interference. The interference is real for males doing moderate continuous cardio but manageable with HIIT.
