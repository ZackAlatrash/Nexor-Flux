## Full stretch under load — train muscles at long length for best growth
tags: exercise, technique, "range-of-motion", rom, "muscle-length", hypertrophy, muscle, growth, selection

Training a muscle at its longest (most-stretched) position under load is one of the most important technique principles for hypertrophy. Multiple studies cited in this narrative review (co-authored by Schoenfeld) show that "training at long muscle lengths elicits more distal hypertrophy than training at short muscle lengths," and that a partial range of motion performed at the **lengthened position** was "potentially superior to full ROM for hypertrophy." In practice: prioritise exercises and rep ranges that place the muscle under load when it is fully stretched — deep squats (quad length), Romanian deadlifts (hamstring length), incline dumbbell curls (bicep length at the bottom), and deficit push-ups or deep dips (chest length). Partial reps at the shortened end of the range (top of a curl, top of a press) are counterproductive for hypertrophy.
