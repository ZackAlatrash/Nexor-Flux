# ISSN Position Stand: HMB — 2024 Update

**Full citation:** Rathmacher JA, Pitchford LM, Stout JR, Townsend JR, Jäger R, Kreider RB, Campbell BI, Kerksick CM, Harty PS, Candow DG, Roberts BM, Arent SM, Kalman DS, Antonio J. International society of sports nutrition position stand: β-hydroxy-β-methylbutyrate (HMB). J Int Soc Sports Nutr. 2024;21(1):2434734. doi: 10.1080/15502783.2024.2434734
**Source URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11740297/
**Access:** open access (CC BY 4.0)

## What this source covers
2024 update to the original 2013 ISSN HMB position stand. Reviews the updated evidence base for HMB's effects on body composition, strength, power, recovery, and safety across populations and training statuses. Includes new data on mechanisms (mTORC1 activation independent of leucine-sensing).

## Key findings & numbers
- Recommended dose: 38 mg/kg body weight/day (= ~3 g/day for an 80 kg person), divided into multiple servings
- Forms studied: HMB-Ca and HMB-FA at doses ranging 1.5–6 g/day
- Body composition in untrained (3–8 weeks, 3 g/day): fat-free mass (FFM) gains of +0.4 to +2.4 kg
- Body composition in trained athletes: Wilson et al. 12-week protocol at 3 g/day reported +5.3 kg LBM and −3.7 kg FM (noted as an outlier; most trained-athlete studies show more modest effects)
- Strength in untrained: 7–45% increases in strength measures
- Strength in trained: mixed results; benefits more pronounced in protocols >6 weeks
- Muscle damage / recovery: reduced CK and LDH across multiple studies; improved soreness and functional recovery following eccentric protocols
- Safety: chronic use safe for at least 1 year; no adverse effects on glucose tolerance or insulin sensitivity; no genotoxicity
- Mechanism: mTORC1 activation independent of leucine-sensing (novel finding vs. 2013 stand)
- HMB-FA bioavailability remains superior to CaHMB in pharmacokinetic studies

## Direct specifics / quotes
> "Daily HMB supplementation (38 mg/kg body weight) in combination with exercise training may improve body composition through increasing lean mass and/or decreasing fat mass with benefits in participants across age, sex, and training status."

## Relevance to a body-recomposition coaching app
- Evidence strength: MODERATE-TO-STRONG for body composition and recovery; MIXED for trained athletes vs. untrained
- Updated 2024 data strengthens the case for HMB across age groups including older adults
- Mechanism via mTORC1 (not just leucine) suggests potential utility even with adequate leucine intake
- 3 g/day in divided doses remains the practical recommendation; duration of at least 6 weeks needed to see consistent effects in trained individuals
- Safe for long-term use; no blood marker concerns
