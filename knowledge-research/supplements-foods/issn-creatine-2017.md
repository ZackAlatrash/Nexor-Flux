# ISSN Position Stand: Creatine Supplementation (2017)

**Full citation:** Kreider RB, Kalman DS, Antonio J, et al. International Society of Sports Nutrition position stand: safety and efficacy of creatine supplementation in exercise, sport, and medicine. J Int Soc Sports Nutr. 2017;14:18. doi: 10.1186/s12970-017-0173-z
**Source URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5469049/
**Access:** open access (CC BY 4.0)

## What this source covers
Comprehensive position stand on creatine monohydrate supplementation covering dosing protocols, performance effects, mechanisms, safety profile, and use in clinical populations based on analysis of over 1,000 studies.

## Key findings & numbers
- Loading protocol: 5 g creatine monohydrate × 4 times/day for 5–7 days (~0.3 g/kg body weight), producing 20–40% increase in muscle creatine stores
- Alternative loading: 3 g/day for 28 days achieves the same saturation more slowly
- Maintenance dose: 3–5 g/day following loading phase; larger athletes may require 5–10 g/day
- Clinical/therapeutic dose: 0.3–0.8 g/kg/day (21–56 g/day for a 70 kg person) used in patient populations
- Performance improvement: 10–20% improvement in high-intensity and repetitive exercise performance
- Lean mass: greater strength and lean mass gains during resistance training vs. placebo
- Safety: studies document safety up to 30 g/day for 5 years; no compelling evidence of renal harm in healthy individuals
- Side effects: weight gain only (water retention and lean mass); no increase in musculoskeletal injury, dehydration, or cramping
- Evidence in untrained vs. trained: benefits consistent across populations; evidence suggests reduced (not increased) injury rates

## Direct specifics / quotes
> "Creatine monohydrate is the most effective ergogenic nutritional supplement currently available to athletes with the intent of increasing high-intensity exercise capacity and lean body mass during training."

> "Short- and long-term supplementation (up to 30 g/day for 5 years) is safe and well-tolerated in healthy individuals and in a number of patient populations."

## Relevance to a body-recomposition coaching app
- Evidence strength: STRONG (1,000+ studies; consistent effects across populations)
- Practical coaching guidance: recommend 3–5 g/day maintenance (no loading required for general use); loading (20 g/day × 5 days) is optional for faster saturation
- Safe to recommend to nearly all healthy adults including adolescents under appropriate conditions
- Weight gain on scale is expected (~1–2 kg) due to water; counsel users not to be alarmed
- Most relevant for strength/power outcomes and lean mass during recomp
