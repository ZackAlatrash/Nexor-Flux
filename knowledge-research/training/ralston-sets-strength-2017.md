# Weekly Set Volume & Strength Gain: Graded Dose-Response (Low ≤5 → Medium 5–9 → High ≥10 Sets)

**Full citation:** Ralston GW, Kilgore L, Wyatt FB, Baker JS. The Effect of Weekly Set Volume on Strength Gain: A Meta-Analysis. Sports Med. 2017;47(12):2585–2601. doi:10.1007/s40279-017-0762-7. PMC5684266. PMID:28755103.
**Source URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5684266/
**Access:** open access (CC BY)

## What this source covers
Meta-analysis of 9 RCTs (223 male subjects; mean age 23.4 years; 1987–2016) examining how weekly training volume (categorized as low ≤5, medium 5–9, high ≥10 sets per muscle per week) affects strength gain.

## Key findings & numbers
- **Combined exercises effect sizes:**
  - Low (≤5 sets/week): ES = 0.82
  - Medium (5–9 sets/week): ES = 0.98
  - High (≥10 sets/week): ES = 1.01
  - HWS vs LWS: ES difference = 0.18 (p = 0.003)
  - MWS vs LWS: ES difference = 0.15 (p = 0.04)
- **Multi-joint exercises:**
  - LWS: ES = 0.81; HWS: ES = 1.00; difference = 0.18 (p = 0.04)
- **Isolation exercises:**
  - LWS: ES = 0.95; HWS: ES = 1.10; difference = 0.23 (p = 0.008)
- Graded dose-response relationship: medium and high volume both superior to low volume for strength.
- Differences between medium and high volume were modest — suggesting major strength gains can occur at moderate volumes.

## Direct specifics / quotes
> "A graded dose–response relationship between weekly sets performed and strength gain."

## Relevance to a body-recomposition coaching app
- Even low volume (≤5 sets/week) produces meaningful strength gains (ES = 0.82) — important for beginner/minimal-dose contexts.
- The jump from low to medium sets is meaningful (p = 0.003); push users toward at least 6–9 sets/week per muscle.
- The jump from medium to high sets is real but smaller — marginal return above ~10 sets/week.
- Isolation exercises show a larger dose-response effect than multi-joint — suggests accessories (curls, lateral raises) benefit more from higher volume.
- Aligns well with Schoenfeld/Ogborn/Krieger (2017) hypertrophy findings: 10+ sets/week is the higher-benefit zone.
