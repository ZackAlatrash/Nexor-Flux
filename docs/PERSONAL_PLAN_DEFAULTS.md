# Personal Plan Defaults

- Calories: 2550 kcal
- Protein: 165 g
- Carbs: 320 g
- Fat: 68 g
- Weight trend threshold: 0.20 kg/week
- Waist increase threshold: 0.5 cm across two weeks
- Minimum adherence: 85%
- Review cadence: 7 days
- Units: metric

The app opens directly to Today. Targets can be changed in Plan and affect Dashboard/Progress immediately through DataStore flows.
