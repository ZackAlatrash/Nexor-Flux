## Minimum sets per week to build muscle — even 4 sets counts
tags: minimum, "minimum-dose", volume, sets, "sets-per-week", hypertrophy, strength, muscle, beginner, "how-many"

A minimum of **4 direct sets per muscle group per week** is enough to produce measurable strength and hypertrophy gains, according to this narrative review co-authored with Schoenfeld. Reaching 10 or more sets per week yields incrementally better results, but 4 sets is the validated floor — far better than zero. Even training each muscle only **once per week** produces similar strength gains to training it 3 times per week when total weekly volume is equated. The load range of 6–15 RM (roughly 65–85 % 1RM) is most efficient; alternatively, 15–40 reps taken to volitional failure also works if heavy equipment is unavailable. For exercise selection, prioritise at least one bilateral lower-body compound (squat or leg press variant), one upper-body pull (row or pull-up), and one upper-body push (press or dip) to maximise returns per unit of time.
