# Training Volume & Hypertrophy: 12–20 Sets/Week is the Evidence-Based Sweet Spot

**Full citation:** Baz-Valle E, Balsalobre-Fernández C, Alix-Fages C, Santos-Concejero J. A Systematic Review of The Effects of Different Resistance Training Volumes on Muscle Hypertrophy. J Hum Kinet. 2022;81:199–210. doi:10.2478/hukin-2022-0017. PMC8884877.
**Source URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8884877/
**Access:** open access (CC BY)

## What this source covers
Systematic review and meta-analysis comparing moderate volume (MV: 12–20 sets/week) versus high volume (HV: >20 sets/week) for muscle hypertrophy in trained men. Includes 7 studies, 6 in meta-analysis, measuring muscle thickness/CSA by ultrasound.

## Key findings & numbers
- Optimal standard recommendation: **12–20 weekly sets per muscle group** for hypertrophy in young trained men.
- Volume classification: Low = <12 sets/week; Moderate (MV) = 12–20; High (HV) = >20.
- **Quadriceps femoris:** No significant difference between MV vs HV (p=0.19, ES −0.2). HV: 9.4–13.7% improvement; MV: 1.6–7.5% improvement (favor HV but not significant).
- **Biceps brachii:** No significant difference MV vs HV (p=0.59, ES −0.1).
- **Triceps brachii:** Significant advantage for HV (p=0.01, ES −0.5). Clear dose-response pattern.
- Higher training frequency (3×/week per muscle) appeared to enable greater benefits from elevated volumes vs 2×/week.
- 62.5% of subjects had better responses when individualizing their training volume.
- Mechanical tension is the primary driver of hypertrophy, increasing muscle protein synthesis via signal transduction.
- Studies: 6–24 weeks duration; participants ≥1 year resistance training experience, ages 18–35 years.

## Direct specifics / quotes
> "A range of 12-20 weekly sets per muscle group may be an optimum standard recommendation for increasing muscle hypertrophy in young, trained men."

## Relevance to a body-recomposition coaching app
- Coach should recommend 12–20 sets/week per muscle for trained users; >20 shows diminishing returns.
- Below 12 sets/week is a suboptimal "low volume" tier — fine for beginners or deload phases.
- Triceps shows a clear dose-response up to high volume; worth flagging for arm-focused goals.
- Volume AND frequency interact: 3×/week per muscle outperforms 2×/week at the same total sets.
- Individual variation is real — allow users to experiment and track response.
