# ISSN Exercise & Sports Nutrition Review Update (Kerksick 2018)

**Full citation:** Kerksick CM, Wilborn CD, Roberts MD, et al. ISSN exercise & sports nutrition review update: research & recommendations. J Int Soc Sports Nutr. 2018;15:38. doi:10.1186/s12970-018-0242-y. PMC6090881.
**Source URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6090881/
**Access:** Open access (CC BY 4.0)

## What this source covers
Comprehensive 2018 update to the ISSN sports nutrition review, covering protein, carbohydrate, fat, calorie requirements for athletes across training volumes, pre/post-exercise nutrition timing, and supplement classification.

## Key findings & numbers

### Protein
- General fitness (sedentary to light activity): **0.8–1.2 g/kg/day**
- Moderate intense training (2–3 h/day, 5–6 days/week): **1.2–2.0 g/kg/day**
- High-volume intense training (3–6 h/day): **1.7–2.2 g/kg/day**
- Resistance-trained individuals in caloric restriction: **2.3–3.1 g/kg FFM/day**
- Meta-analysis consensus (Morton et al., 49 studies, 1,863 participants): optimal at **1.62 g/kg/day**
- Acute dose per meal: **0.25–0.55 g/kg** or **20–40 g** absolute, containing **700–3,000 mg leucine**, every 3–4 hours
- Older adults may require **40 g doses** vs. 20 g for younger individuals

### Calories
- General fitness (30–40 min, 3×/week): **25–35 kcal/kg/day** (approx. 1,800–2,400 kcal/day)
- Moderate training: **40–70 kcal/kg/day** (2,000–7,000 kcal/day)
- High-volume training: **600–1,200 kcal/hour** during exercise
- Elite cyclists (Tour de France): up to **12,000 kcal/day**
- Weight loss phase: **~750–1,000 kcal/day deficit** (implied from context)

### Carbohydrate
- General fitness: **3–5 g/kg/day** (45–55% of calories)
- Moderate training: **5–8 g/kg/day**
- High-volume training: **8–10 g/kg/day**
- Pre-competition carb-loading: additional **200–300 g/day** for 2–3 days with 30–50% training taper
- During exercise: **0.7 g/kg/hour** in 6–8% solution; optimal oxidation ~**60 g/hour**; maltodextrin:fructose ~1.2:1.0 ratio for up to 100 g/hour
- Immediate post-exercise: **1 g/kg carbohydrate + 0.5 g/kg protein** within 30 min
- Rapid glycogen restoration: **1.2 g/kg/hour**

### Fat
- General athletes: **~30% of daily calories**
- High-volume training: safely up to **50% of daily calories**
- Weight loss/fat reduction phase: **0.5–1 g/kg/day** (~20% of total calories)
- Ketogenic diet: 70–80% calories from fat, 20–25% protein (2.0–2.5 g/kg/day), 10–40 g CHO/day — "limited and mixed evidence" for athletic performance

## Direct specifics / quotes
> "It is often very difficult for larger athletes and athletes engaged in high volume/intense training to be able to eat enough food, on a daily basis, to meet caloric needs."

> "For years, high-fat diets have been used by athletes with the majority of evidence showing no ergogenic benefit and consistent gastrointestinal challenges."

## Relevance to a body-recomposition coaching app
- Use the population-stratified tables directly for setting macro targets based on user's training volume.
- Protein ceiling for practical purposes: 2.2 g/kg/day for most users gaining muscle; up to 3.1 g/kg FFM during active cuts.
- For weight loss, fat should not drop below 0.5 g/kg/day to protect hormonal function.
- This review consolidates the ISSN consensus and is the most complete single-source reference for macro ranges by training level.
