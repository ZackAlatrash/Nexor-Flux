# ISSN Position Stand: Beta-Alanine (2015)

**Full citation:** Trexler ET, Smith-Ryan AE, Stout JR, Hoffman JR, Wilborn CD, Sale C, Kreider RB, Jäger R, Earnest CP, Bannock L, Campbell B, Kalman D, Ziegenfuss TN, Antonio J. International society of sports nutrition position stand: Beta-Alanine. J Int Soc Sports Nutr. 2015;12:30. doi: 10.1186/s12970-015-0090-y
**Source URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4501114/
**Access:** open access (CC BY 4.0)

## What this source covers
ISSN position stand on beta-alanine (BA) supplementation covering dosing, duration, mechanisms (carnosine synthesis), exercise-type specificity, paresthesia management, and safety. Based on meta-analyses and peer-reviewed literature through March 2015.

## Key findings & numbers
- Dose: 4–6 g/day divided into multiple doses of ≤2 g each (large boluses >800 mg trigger paresthesia)
- Duration to significant effect: minimum 2–4 weeks; saturation continues with extended use
- Muscle carnosine increase: ~20–30% after 2 weeks; 40–60% after 4 weeks; up to 80% after 10 weeks
- Dosing frequency: distributed doses (e.g., 1.6 g × 3–4 per day) or sustained-release formulations recommended to minimize tingling
- Paresthesia threshold: >800 mg in non-sustained-release form triggers tingling; resolves within 60–90 minutes; benign
- Exercise types that benefit most: high-intensity exercise lasting 1–4 minutes (e.g., 400–1500 m runs, rowing, combat sports)
- Physical working capacity at fatigue threshold (PWCFT): improved 14–20% in younger adults, more pronounced in older subjects (55–92 years)
- Aerobic exercise up to ~25 minutes: modest benefit; limited evidence for exercise >25 minutes
- Open end-point tasks (time-to-exhaustion): greater benefit than fixed end-point tests
- Safety: well-tolerated in healthy populations at recommended doses; no clinically significant adverse effects beyond paresthesia

## Direct specifics / quotes
> "Daily supplementation with 4 to 6 g of beta-alanine for at least 2 to 4 weeks has been shown to improve exercise performance, with more pronounced effects in open end-point tasks/time trials lasting 1 to 4 min in duration."

## Relevance to a body-recomposition coaching app
- Evidence strength: STRONG for 1–4 minute high-intensity efforts; limited for longer endurance or pure strength work
- Practical coaching guidance: 4–6 g/day in divided doses ≤2 g; sustained-release avoids paresthesia if tolerated
- Coaches should pre-warn users about the tingling side effect to prevent unnecessary concern and discontinuation
- Most relevant during high-intensity interval training (HIIT) phases; less relevant for steady-state cardio
- Can be stacked safely with creatine and caffeine (common in pre-workout formulas)
