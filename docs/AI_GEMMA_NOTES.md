# AI/Gemma Notes

Gemma is intentionally not part of MVP decision-making.

## Allowed later
- Summarize already-calculated weekly stats.
- Explain why the deterministic rule engine returned a verdict.
- Draft meal ideas from local saved foods and macro targets.

## Not allowed
- Change calorie targets directly.
- Override verdicts.
- Infer missing measurements.
- Use hosted inference, online search, or account-backed services.

The current `GemmaInsightService` reports that AI summaries are unavailable in the MVP and keeps the boundary explicit.
