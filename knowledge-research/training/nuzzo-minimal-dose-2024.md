# Minimal Dose for Strength: 5 Evidence-Based Strategies That All Work

**Full citation:** Nuzzo JL, Pinto MD, Kirk BJC, Nosaka K. Resistance Exercise Minimal Dose Strategies for Increasing Muscle Strength in the General Population: an Overview. Sports Med. 2024. doi:10.1007/s40279-024-02009-0. PMC11127831.
**Source URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11127831/
**Access:** open access (CC BY)

## What this source covers
Overview article identifying and reviewing 5 minimal dose resistance exercise strategies for improving muscle strength in the general population. Addresses the major barrier of "perceived lack of time."

## Key findings & numbers
- **~80% of individuals** fail to meet current resistance exercise guidelines — "perceived lack of time" is the most cited barrier.
- Five minimal dose strategies, all evidence-supported:

  1. **"Weekend Warrior" (once-weekly):**
     - Minimum: 1 session/week with 4+ exercises.
     - Strength gains: **6–74% improvements** depending on study.
     - "Most strongly supported by current research."

  2. **Single-set training:**
     - 1 set per exercise, 7–10 exercises, ≥2 days/week; ~30 minutes/session.
     - Strength gains: **2–39%** across studies; mean ~20% gain.
     - Comparable to multi-set for many populations.
     - "Most strongly supported by current research."

  3. **Exercise "snacks":**
     - Brief daily bouts (few minutes), 5–7 days/week distributed through the day.
     - Improves sit-to-stand and leg press power in older adults.
     - "Emerging concept with promising results."

  4. **Practicing the strength test (isometric MVC):**
     - Daily maximal contractions, 5–7 days/week, <3 minutes.
     - Strength gains: **10–92%** for isometric tests.
     - "Most time-efficient way to achieve" isometric strength increases.
     - Proof-of-concept stage only.

  5. **Eccentric minimal dose:**
     - Low weekly volumes of eccentric-only reps; 1–7 days/week.
     - Strength gains: **0–59%** depending on frequency.
     - Daily maximal eccentrics (5 days/week) outperform concentrated weekly volumes.

## Direct specifics / quotes
> "One session of resistance exercise per week...improves muscle strength and other measures of physical fitness."

> "Small volumes of resistance exercise [are] better for muscle strength than no resistance exercise at all."

> "Approximately 80% of individuals fail to meet current resistance exercise guidelines."

## Relevance to a body-recomposition coaching app
- "Weekend warrior" framing is powerful for time-crunched users — validate even 1 session/week as meaningful.
- Single-set training (~30 min, 2×/week) is a legitimate starting point for sedentary users.
- Progressive messaging: "1 set is better than 0; 3 sets is better than 1; 10+ sets/week is optimal."
- Exercise snacks (short bouts through the day) may be an innovative feature — quick bodyweight sessions between meals.
- For users who say they "don't have time," these strategies provide real scientific backing for accessible options.
