## Fiber is the strongest dietary predictor of weight loss and adherence
tags: fiber, fibre, satiety, full, hunger, appetite, adherence, weight, loss, calories, diet
In the POUNDS Lost RCT (345 adults, 750 kcal/day deficit, 6 months), dietary fiber was **the most influential predictor of weight loss** among all dietary variables tested (partial R² = 0.17). Every **3.7 g/day increase in fiber** predicted **1.4 kg of additional weight loss** over 6 months. Participants in the highest fiber-increase group (+8–29 g/day) lost **10.3 kg** vs. 5.8 kg in the lowest group. Meeting the Adequate Intake for fiber (25 g/day women, 38 g/day men) was associated with **2.94× higher odds of diet adherence** (99% CI: 1.56–5.55). Fiber is not just a health metric — it is an adherence lever.

## Fiber targets and how to hit them
tags: fiber, fibre, satiety, full, hunger, how-much, calories, calorie, diet, adherence
The U.S. Adequate Intake for fiber is **25 g/day for women** and **38 g/day for men** (14 g per 1,000 kcal consumed). Average U.S. intake is only ~15 g/day. A 3–4 g/day increase is meaningful — achievable by adding one serving of legumes, an extra serving of vegetables, or a tablespoon of psyllium husk. High-fiber foods that also contribute protein include lentils, chickpeas, edamame, and black beans — practical double-duty choices for recomp goals.

## Soluble fiber is most potent for hunger suppression
tags: fiber, fibre, hunger, appetite, satiety, full, fullness, craving, diet
A meta-analysis of 17 RCTs (Salleh 2019) ranked soluble fiber types by satiety effect: **guar gum** (effect size −0.90) was strongest, followed by **beta-glucan** (−0.44, found in oats), **alginate** (−0.42), polydextrose (−0.36), and pectin (−0.26). Soluble fibers increase digesta viscosity, delay gastric emptying, and extend fullness signals. Liquid delivery (smoothies with psyllium, oat-based drinks) produced stronger satiety than solid form. Practical picks: oats, psyllium husk, lentils, and legumes are the most accessible high-soluble-fiber foods for someone managing hunger on a calorie deficit.
